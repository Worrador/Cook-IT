## App details

- **App name:** Cook-IT
- **Short description:** Organize recipes, track meals, and optionally sync your recipe book with Google Drive.
- **Full description:**

  Cook-IT is a simple, personal recipe book for the meals you want to make again.

  Save recipe links and notes, pin favourites, and keep track of meals you have cooked. Your recipe book works on-device by default. If you choose to connect Google Drive, Cook-IT can save and sync your recipe workbook with your own Drive account.

  Features:
  - Save recipe names, links, and personal notes
  - Pin favourite recipes for quick access
  - Track cooked meals and cooking history
  - Optional Google Drive backup and sync
  - No ads and no in-app purchases
