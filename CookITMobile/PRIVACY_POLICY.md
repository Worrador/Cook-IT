# Cook-IT Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [date]

Cook-IT is provided by **[developer or legal business name]**. This policy explains how Cook-IT handles information when you use the app.

## Information we handle

Cook-IT stores the recipes, links, notes, favourites, and cooking history that you enter on your device.

Google Drive sync is optional. If you choose to connect Google Drive, Cook-IT requests your Google account identity and permission to create, read, and update the Cook-IT recipe workbook in your Google Drive. The app uses this information only to authenticate you and provide the sync feature.

## How information is used and shared

Cook-IT uses your recipe data to provide the recipe-book, cooking-history, and optional sync features. It does not sell recipe data or use it for advertising. When you enable Google Drive sync, your selected data is transmitted to Google to store in your Google Drive account, subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Recipe data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google Drive data remains in your Google Drive account until you delete the relevant workbook. You can disconnect Google Drive from within the app; this removes the app's locally stored sign-in information but does not delete the workbook from your Drive.

## Security

Cook-IT uses the Google sign-in flow and HTTPS connections for Google Drive communication. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Cook-IT is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing. Do not use Google Drive sync on behalf of a child without appropriate consent.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy with a new effective date.
